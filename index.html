About Me

Dedicated to Medicine, Education & Discovery

I am a Professor of Medicine based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with over two decades of experience in clinical practice, academic teaching, and biomedical research. My work bridges the laboratory and the lecture hall — translating cutting-edge science into practical clinical knowledge for tomorrow's doctors.

My primary research focus lies in the bioactive components of human breast milk — including immunoglobulins, lactoferrin, oligosaccharides, and growth factors — and their profound implications for neonatal health, immunity, and long-term development. I am deeply committed to postgraduate medical education and advancing evidence-based pharmacology teaching.

Research Areas

Current Research & Interests

🍼

Breast Milk Bioactive Components

Investigating lactoferrin, secretory IgA, HMOs, and growth factors in human breast milk and their roles in neonatal immunity, gut microbiome development, and long-term metabolic health.

💊

Clinical Pharmacology & Drug Safety

Research into adverse drug reactions, drug-drug interactions, pharmacovigilance, and rational prescribing practices in the Indian clinical context.

🔬

Neonatal Nutrition & Immunology

Exploring the immunoprotective properties of colostrum and mature breast milk, including cytokine profiles, microRNA content, and protective effects against neonatal infections.

📊

Evidence-Based Medicine

Systematic reviews and meta-analyses in clinical pharmacology and maternal-neonatal medicine to guide clinical decision-making and therapeutic protocols.

🧬

Milk Proteomics & Metabolomics

Advanced profiling of breast milk protein and metabolite composition across lactation stages and their correlation with infant developmental outcomes.

📚

Medical Education Research

Developing and evaluating innovative PG teaching methodologies including case-based learning, flipped classrooms, and competency-based pharmacology curricula.

Postgraduate Teaching

Academic & Teaching Philosophy

01

Case-Based Pharmacology Teaching

Real clinical cases anchoring drug mechanism, dosing, ADRs, and therapeutic decision-making for MD/MS residents in a contextually rich format.

02

PG Research Mentorship

Guiding postgraduate students through protocol development, IRB submissions, data analysis, dissertation writing, and international publication.

03

Drug Rational Use Workshops

Conducting workshops on rational prescribing, pharmacovigilance reporting, and antimicrobial stewardship for junior residents and interns.

04

Bedside Clinical Teaching

Structured bedside rounds integrating pathophysiology, pharmacotherapy, and clinical reasoning for senior residents across medical specialties.

05

Journal Club & Critical Appraisal

Weekly sessions training PG students to critically evaluate clinical trials, systematic reviews, and pharmacological studies from high-impact journals.

06

Examination & Assessment

Designing competency-based assessments, OSCE stations, and structured viva examinations aligned with NMC PG curriculum frameworks.
